How it works

One reserve. Your name on the dollar.

Tender is a Solana program. It runs the reserve, the minting and the yield math. You pick the name and where the yield goes. Nobody can change that but you.

01

Name it

Pick a ticker like BONKUSD. Tender creates a Token-2022 mint with your name and symbol on-chain. The program is its only mint authority, and there is no freeze key.

02

Mint and redeem at par

Anyone deposits 1 USDC and gets 1 of your dollar. Anyone burns 1 and gets 1 USDC back. No fee, no spread, no oracle in the way. Redemptions can’t be paused.

03

Route the yield

The reserve earns on tokenized Treasuries. Your share, sized by your supply, goes to up to four routes: USDC to a wallet, or freshly minted dollars to a wallet.

The reserve

Every dollar stands on the same pile.

All community dollars share one reserve of liquid USDC and tokenized Treasuries. Pooling means a small community gets the same Treasury yield as a big one, and redemptions draw on the whole pile. The program won’t let the allocator move liquid USDC below a set floor.

Who it’s for

A dollar for anyone with a crowd.
